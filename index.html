AI Composer

A neural network that learned to write music by studying Bach's chorales. Pick a band and a key, and it writes a brand new piece in four parts, note by note, right in your browser.

    How it was built

      Music: the Bach chorales in the music21 corpus (the chorales themselves are in the public domain). Instrument sounds: the FluidR3 General MIDI soundfont by Frank Wen, rendered by gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts, licensed CC BY 3.0. Changes made: every third note kept, trimmed and converted to mono.

      Code: github.com/s-milly/ai-composer

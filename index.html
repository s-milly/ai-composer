AI Composer

Describe the song you want to hear. One model reads your description, another writes the music note by note, and a band plays it on stage. If you would rather write it yourself, the Song Maker lets you place every note.

        The notes

          Pick a track, choose its instrument, and click the grid to place notes. Drag to the right while placing a note to make it longer, and click a note to remove it. The other tracks show as dotted outlines so you can line up chords. No AI is involved here: every note is yours.

          How it was built

            Descriptions: MusicCaps by Google (Agostinelli et al., 2023), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; the description reader trained on it is shared under the same license. Word vectors: GloVe (Pennington, Socher and Manning, 2014), public domain. Music: the Bach chorales in the music21 corpus (the chorales themselves are in the public domain). Instrument sounds: the FluidR3 General MIDI soundfont by Frank Wen, rendered by gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts, licensed CC BY 3.0. Changes made: every third note kept, trimmed and converted to mono.

            Code: github.com/s-milly/ai-composer
